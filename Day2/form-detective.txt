Day 2 - Form Detective

GET Method:
- Sends form data through the URL.
- Submitted values can be seen in the address bar.
- Suitable for search forms and non-sensitive data.

POST Method:
- Sends form data in the request body.
- Data does not appear in the URL.
- Suitable for submitting sensitive or larger amounts of data.
- POST is not automatically secure; HTTPS is needed.

Important:
- Every form input should have a name attribute.
- The form method decides how data is submitted.
- The action attribute specifies where form data is sent.
